# Precision medicine

In collaboration with APHP Paris-Saclay, I develop personalized computational models of whole-body homeostasis. My research leverages high-dimensional data from single PET scans to reconstruct physiological photographs.

*This research is conducted in parallel of my activities at the ENSAE Paris or at the ENS Paris-Saclay.*
